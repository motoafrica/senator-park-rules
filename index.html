Management Rule — PMR 9–18

1. Trustees & Board Authority

1.1 Election and Composition: The Body Corporate shall elect a Board of Trustees at each Annual General Meeting (AGM) to conduct and oversee the administration of the scheme.

1.2 Quorums and Voting: A quorum for general meetings requires owners holding at least 25% of total votes. Trustee meetings require a majority of elected trustees.

Management Rule — Operational

2. Building Manager & Managing Agent

2.1 Delegated Authority: The Building Manager serves as the primary on-site administrator under trustee oversight to enforce building rules, direct contractors, and oversee daily maintenance.

2.2 Infractions and Reporting: All resident disputes, rule non-compliance, access control issues, and common property damage must be formally logged with the Building Manager's office.

Management / Finance — PMR 25

3. Levies, Finance & Participation Quotas

3.1 Due Date: Monthly levy contributions are payable strictly in advance on or before the 1st day of each month.

3.2 Participation Quotas: Calculated per registered section (Residential: 0.5484%, Commercial: 7.8688%).

Conduct Rule — Security

6. Security, Access & Deliveries

6.1 Access Tags & Biometrics: Access credentials are non-transferable. Sub-letting or lending access tags to non-residents is strictly prohibited.

6.2 Couriers & Deliveries: Food and parcel delivery couriers (Uber Eats, Mr D, fast food drivers) are strictly prohibited from entering building towers. All residents must meet delivery personnel at ground level.
