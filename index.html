CONDUCT RULES OF THE SENATOR PARK BODY CORPORATE

SS NO. 116/1981 AND SS 137/1982

Conduct Rules — Part 1

Introductory

1. Heading: Conduct rules as prescribed, with amendments and additions, in terms of section 10(2)(b) of the Sectional Titles Schemes Management Act, 2011 (Act No. 8 of 2011).

2. Interpretation:

(1) (a) "access tag" means a device assigned to the owners, occupiers and employees of the body corporate which allows that owner, occupier or employee to deactivate the electronic locks of the doors, gates or turnstiles;

(b) "adjudicator" means an adjudicator acting in terms of the Community Schemes Ombud Service Act, 2011 (Act No. 9 of 2011);

(c) "auditor" means a person accredited to perform an audit in terms of the Auditing Professions Act, 2005 (Act No. 26 of 2005);

(d) "body corporate" means the body corporate of this sectional title scheme, Senator Park, as defined in the Act;

(e) "commercial section" means section 1 of the scheme, situated on the ground floor of the building, including any subdivision or part of the commercial section;

(f) "common property" in relation to the scheme means (i) the land included in the scheme; (ii) all parts of the building that do not form part of a section; and (iii) land referred to in section 5(1)(d) of the Act;

(g) "Community Schemes Ombud Service" means the service established in terms of the Community Scheme Ombud Service Act, 2011 (Act No. 9 of 2011);

(h) "directives" means the directives which may be issued by the trustees in terms of these rules, the management rules, legislation or statutory regulations;

(i) "exclusive use area" means a part or parts of the common property that is linked to a section for the exclusive use of owner(s) or occupier(s);

(j) "heavy vehicle" for the purpose of rule 22(4) means any vehicle that weighs in excess of 2,500 kilograms;

(k) "guidelines" means the building and aesthetic guidelines that may be compiled and amended from time to time by the trustees;

(l) "local authority" means the City of Cape Town or its successors in title or assigns;

(m) "managing agent" means any person who provides scheme management services to a body corporate for reward, whether monetary or otherwise;

(n) "member" means a member of the body corporate;

(o) "nuisance" means any conduct, act or omission or condition, which, in the reasonable opinion of the trustees, is offensive, injurious or dangerous to health, materially interferes with the ordinary comfort, convenience, peace or quiet of, or which adversely affects the safety of, an owner or occupier;

(p) "occupier" or "tenant" means the lessee or resident of a residential section, or the lessee or employee of the commercial section;

(q) "owner" in relation to a unit or section means the person in whose name the unit is registered in a deeds registry;

(r) "renovation" means an internal redecoration or refurbishment of the existing décor or interior of a section;

(s) "scheme" means (i) Senator Park Sectional Title Scheme SS 116/1981; (ii) SS 137/1982; (iii) SS 191/1982;

(t) "section" means a section shown as such on the sectional plan of the scheme;

(u) "Sectional Titles Act" means the Sectional Titles Act, 1986 (Act No. 95 of 1986);

(v) "security officer" means the security personnel of the security service provider contracted by the body corporate;

(w) "structural alteration" means an alteration which is of a permanent nature and alters the form, structure or essential framework of the section or building inside or outside;

(x) "the Act" means the Sectional Titles Schemes Management Act 8 of 2011;

(y) "the Regulations" means the Sectional Titles Schemes Management Regulations, 2016.

3. Amendment and binding nature: (1) The body corporate may substitute, amend, repeal, or add to the conduct rules subject to section 10 of the Act. (2) An owner must take all reasonable steps to ensure compliance by tenants, guests, visitors, and contractors. (3) Occupiers are obliged to comply notwithstanding any lease terms.

4. Directives and guidelines: (1) Trustees may issue directives, guidelines, application forms, and procedures. (2) Trustees may issue aesthetic guidelines. (3) Directives must not conflict with rules or statutory laws.

5. Written consent of the trustees: (1) Applications must be made in writing. (2) Trustees may attach conditions or withdraw consent upon written notice. (3) No work/action may proceed until written consent is received.

6. Written notices or requests: (1) In prescribed trustee format. (2) Served by hand, registered post (5 days), or email. (3) Delivered to trustees by hand or designated email.

7. Relaxation of these rules: No indulgence constitutes a waiver or precedent.

8. Contravention of these rules: Trustees may enter sections to remedy contraventions, bring court applications, instruct attorneys, institute arbitration, or apply to the CSOS Ombud.

9. Liability for damage, legal costs and other costs: (1) Owners liable for damages to common property caused by occupants/guests. (2) Owners liable for all reasonable legal fees incurred by body corporate on an attorney-client scale.

10. Disclaimer: Persons use premises at own risk. Body corporate is not liable for injury, loss, or property damage directly or indirectly sustained.

Conduct Rules — Part 2

Safety, Security & Access

11. Fire safety: (1) Fire equipment and alarms may only be used for fighting fires/emergencies. (2) Owners must inform tenants of fire escape routes. (3) Storage of flammable liquids prohibited except retail domestic containers. (4) Gas cylinders require prior written trustee consent and compliance certificates. (5) Fire hazards must be eliminated immediately upon notice. (6) No smoking or open flames anywhere on common property. (7) Fire escape routes, hallways, and stairwells must be kept clear at all times.

12. Security: (1) Ensure doors/gates close properly. No unauthorized access permitted. (2) Tampering with CCTV, doors, or gates prohibited. (3) Section security gates must be kept shut; warning issued prior to removal if blocking hallways. (4) Obstruction, verbal abuse, or disobedience toward security officers strictly prohibited. (5) Complaints against security must be lodged in writing with the building manager/trustees.

13. Registration of owners, occupants and agents: (1) Owners must notify body corporate of change of occupancy prior to occurrence. (2) Submissions must include full tenant documentation and contact details. (3) Registration complete upon written confirmation. Unregistered persons treated as visitors.

14. Access tags and codes: (1) 1 tag per owner, 1 tag per section. Additional tags at cost price. (2) Unauthorized tags confiscated/deactivated. (3) Lockbox tags require 24-hour advance written notice to building manager. (4) Lost/stolen tags must be reported immediately. (5) Gate codes restricted to building manager and authorized parking bay owners. (6) Body corporate reserves right to deactivate tags/codes for security reasons.

15. Access of visitors: (1) Unregistered visitors without tags must be accompanied by registered resident or approved by building manager. (2) Building manager may authorize contractors, emergency, and official personnel. (3) Rule-breaking visitors subject to blacklisting and entry denial.

16. Delivery of items: (1) Couriers & Food Delivery: Delivery drivers (Uber Eats, Mr D, etc.) are strictly prohibited inside the building. Residents must meet drivers outside at ground level. (2) Body corporate staff will not receive parcels. (3) Post collected from reception post boxes. (4) Furniture/building material moves require prior arrangement with building manager and mandatory use of the goods lift.

Conduct Rules — Part 3

Use of Common Property & Exclusive Use

17. Behaviour of owners, occupiers and visitors on common property: (1) No defacing common property, nuisance, obstruction, illegal acts, or reputation damage to Senator Park. (2) Prostitution, drug dealing/use strictly prohibited; leases must be terminated immediately upon trustee instruction. (3) No alcohol consumption on common property. (4) No smoking/vaping on any common property inside or outside. (5) No fireworks/explosives. (6) No firearms except self-defence. (7) No lift overloading. (8) No plant damage. (9) No littering or throwing items from windows. (10) No skateboards, rollerblades, or bicycles on common property. (11) No ball games. (12) No disruptive hobbies. (13) Children must be supervised. (14) No auctions/fetations without written consent. (15) No animal slaughtering without written trustee consent.

18. Refuse disposal and recycling: (1) No waste left outside designated bins. No dumping of oil/paint down drains. (2) Refuse must be securely bagged, non-dripping, and placed inside designated bins with lids closed. Notify building manager of overflow.

19. Protection devices of sections on common property: Security screens/gates require prior written trustee approval regarding design, style, and color, maintained at owner expense.

20. Appearance from outside of sections and exclusive use areas: (1) No external changes without written consent. (2) No external washing lines; laundry must not be visible outside. No signs, billboards, satellite dishes, or air conditioners without trustee approval.

21. Exclusive use areas (parking bays and storerooms): (1)-(4) Allocated per scheme plan layout. (5) Exclusive use rights remain body corporate property unless notarially ceded. (6) Sub-leasing exclusive use areas for profit is prohibited. (7) Transferred automatically upon section sale. (8) Can be ceded back to body corporate upon written notice. (11) Bays strictly for vehicle parking; storerooms strictly for storage. (13) Kept clean by owner. (14) Maintenance costs defrayed by linked section owner contributions.

22. Vehicles: Speed limit 10 km/h. Parked strictly in designated bays. Dripping oil/fluid must be cleaned immediately. Towing/clamping enforced for unauthorized parking at violator's cost.

Conduct Rules — Part 4

Use of Sections & Renovations

23. Behaviour in sections: Quiet hours enforced 23:00 to 08:00 daily. Music, TV, shouting kept to reasonable levels.

24. Keeping of animals: No pets allowed without prior written trustee consent. Approved pets must be leashed on common property; waste cleaned immediately.

25. Eradication of pests: Section owners must keep sections free of timber borers, cockroaches, rodents, and pests.

26. Renovations & alterations to sections: Work allowed only weekdays 08:00–17:00. No noise on Sundays/Holidays. Structural changes require trustee approval, deposit, and municipal plans. Debris removed daily via goods lift.

Conduct Rules — Part 5

Financial Obligations of Owners

27. Levies of the body corporate: Payable in advance on the 1st of each month. Interest charged on overdue accounts at prescribed rates plus legal recovery costs.

MANAGEMENT RULES OF THE SENATOR PARK BODY CORPORATE

SS NO. 116/1981 AND SS 137/1982

Management Rules — Part 1

Introductory & Service Addresses

1. Heading: Management rules as prescribed in terms of section 10(2)(a) of the Sectional Titles Schemes Management Act, 2011.

2. Interpretation: Definitions for adjudicator, administrator, auditor, CSOS, estimated cost, expected life, major capital item, managing agent, member, primary section, reserve funds, Sectional Titles Act, service address, the Act, Regulations, and utility section.

3. Amendment and binding nature: Amendments subject to section 10 of Act. Members must ensure tenant compliance.

4. Service addresses: (1) Domicilium citandi et executandi determined by trustees. (2)-(4) CSOS notification required. (5) Member service address is registered section or written email notification.

Management Rules — Part 2

Trustees & Fundamental Obligations

5. Fiduciary position of trustees: Must act honestly, in good faith, avoid conflicts, and refrain from personal economic gain.

6. Fundamental obligations: Educate on scheme rules, obtain advice, attend AGM and trustee meetings, exercise independent judgment.

7. Requirements for office & disqualification: Minimum 5 trustees. Disqualified if insolvent, convicted of theft/fraud, of unsound mind, or in levy arrears following court judgment.

8. Nomination, election and replacement: Written nominations delivered 48 hours prior to AGM. Commercial section owner entitled to 1 trustee seat.

9. Payment and indemnity: Reimbursement for actual expenses. Member trustees unremunerated unless special resolution passed. Body corporate indemnifies trustees acting in good faith.

Management Rules — Part 3

Trustee Meetings & Decisions

10. General powers and duties: Administer scheme, apply approved budgets, compile minutes within 7 days.

11. Validity of actions: Documents bound only when signed by 2 trustees or 1 trustee + managing agent (clearance certs by 2 trustees or agent).

12. Calling and attendance of meetings: 7 days written notice. Monthly meetings mandatory. Quorum is 50% of trustees.

13. Chairperson: Elected by trustees at first meeting post-AGM.

14. Quorum: 50% of trustees. Interim resolutions adopted if no quorum, confirmed at next meeting.

15. Voting: Majority vote. Chair has casting vote on ties.

16. Mandatory first actions of new trustees: Provide rules copy, calculate member levy contributions within 14 days of AGM budget approval, execute mandatory checks.

17. Mandatory monthly actions: Monthly check of bank statements, levy collection arrears, reserve fund allocations, and building manager operational reports.

Management Rules — Part 4

Owner Meetings & AGMs

18. Notice: 14 days written notice specifying place, date, hour, agenda, budgets, and proxy form.

19. Annual and Special General Meetings: AGM held within 4 months of financial year-end. SGMs called on request of 25% quota owners or 25% bondholders.

20-22. Quorum & Voting: Quorum is 25% of scheme total participation quota. Voting rights restricted if levies are in arrears under court/CSOS judgment.

Management Rules — Part 5

Financial Management & Funds

23-28. Funds, Budgets & Audits: Separate Administrative and Reserve funds required. Statutory reserve maintenance plan updated annually. Insurance replacement schedules approved at AGM. Annual financial audit mandatory.

Management Rules — Parts 6 & 7

Administrative & Physical Management

29-30. Managing Agents: Appointed under written contract; trustee oversight mandated.

31-33. Physical Maintenance & Geysers: Body corporate maintains common property elements. Owners maintain section interiors and exclusive-use water geysers serving solely their section.
