Senator Park Body Corporate

SS No. 116/1981 & SS 137/1982 • 66 Keerom Street, Cape Town

CSOS Approved
Management PMR 9 - 18

Trustees & General Meetings

  • • Trustee Board: Elected annually at the AGM to oversee day-to-day operations and enforce rules.
  • • Quorum: A quorum for general meetings requires owners holding at least 25% of the total votes.
  • • Voting Rights: Votes are calculated according to participation quotas, restricted if levies are in arrears.
Management PMR 25 / SMRA 31

Levy Contributions & Quotas

  • • Due Date: Levies are payable monthly in advance on or before the 1st day of each month.
  • • Participation Quotas: Residential: 0.5484%; Commercial: 7.8688%.
  • • Reserve Fund: A statutory reserve fund is maintained for long-term maintenance and capital expenses.
Management PMR 30 & 68

Common Property & Geysers

  • • Body Corporate Duty: Maintenance and repair of all common property (facade, lifts, structural elements).
  • • Owner Duty: Owners must maintain their sections and interior installations, including water geysers and pipes serving solely their section.
  • • Alterations: No structural changes affecting common property may occur without trustee written consent.
Management PMR 26

Financial Records & Inspection

  • • Bookkeeping: The body corporate must keep proper accounting records of all income, expenditure, assets, and liabilities.
  • • Inspection: Registered owners and bondholders may inspect financial records upon written notice to the managing agent.
Conduct Rule 4.2

Short-Term Occupancy Limits

  • • Minimum Stay: 2 nights absolute minimum. 1-night bookings are strictly prohibited.
  • • Occupancy Cap: Maximum of 2 adults per residential section to prevent overcrowding.
  • • Guest Registry: Tenants must register full guest details with security prior to arrival.
Conduct Rule 5.1

Quiet Hours & Nuisance

  • • Quiet Hours: Enforced daily between 23:00 and 08:00. No loud music or instruments.
  • • Sundays & Holidays: Heavy drilling, hammering, or noisy construction is strictly prohibited.
  • • Common Property: Smoking, vaping, and alcohol consumption are banned in corridors and lifts.
Conduct Rule 3.3

Deliveries & Access Control

  • • Couriers: Food and package delivery personnel (Uber Eats, Mr D, etc.) are not permitted inside the building towers. Residents must meet drivers at ground level.
  • • Access Tags: Biometric or tag access required for all occupants; cloning or lending tags results in deactivation.
Conduct Rule 2.1

Pets & Animals

  • • Approval: No pets are allowed without prior written consent from the Trustees.
  • • Leash Rule: All approved pets must be kept on a short leash when traversing common hallways or ground areas.
