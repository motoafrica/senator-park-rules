Senator Park Body Corporate

SS No. 116/1981 & SS 137/1982 • 66 Keerom Street, Cape Town

CSOS Approved (32 Pages)
Management PMR 9 - 18

Trustees & General Meetings

  • • Election & Number: Trustees are elected at each AGM. Minimum of 3 trustees required to manage the scheme.
  • • Quorum (Meetings): General meetings require owners holding at least 25% of total votes to constitute a quorum.
  • • Trustee Meetings: Quorum for trustee meetings is a majority of elected trustees. Decisions require majority vote.
  • • Voting Restrictions: Owners in arrears with levies or in breach of conduct rules forfeit voting rights at AGMs/SGMs.
Full Governance & Powers
Management / Finance PMR 25 / SMRA 31

Levies, Budgets & Participation Quotas

  • • Monthly Due Date: Contributions to the administrative and reserve funds are due on the 1st day of each month.
  • • Participation Quotas (PQ): Residential sections PQ: 0.5484%; Commercial sections PQ: 7.8688%.
  • • Reserve Fund: Maintained strictly in accordance with statutory requirements for long-term capital maintenance.
  • • Interest & Legal Costs: Overdue levies accrue interest at prime rate plus legal recovery costs on an attorney-client scale.
Financial Obligations
Management / Maintenance PMR 30 & 68

Common Property & Geysers

  • • Body Corporate Duty: Maintain, repair, and keep common property (lifts, structural walls, corridors, roofs) in a serviceable state.
  • • Owner Duty (Geysers & Pipes): Owners are solely responsible for interior maintenance, including water geysers and pipes serving exclusively their section.
  • • Exclusive Use Areas: Owners allocated exclusive use bays/balconies must maintain them clean and structurally sound at their expense.
  • • Alterations: No owner may paint exterior walls, alter balcony enclosures, or modify common property without unanimous/special trustee consent.
Structural & Fixture Duties
Management PMR 26

Financial Records & Audit

  • • Books of Account: Trustees must keep proper accounting records reflecting all assets, liabilities, income, and expenses.
  • • Annual Financial Statements: Prepared within 4 months of financial year-end for presentation at the AGM.
  • • Inspection Rights: Registered owners, bondholders, and CSOS officials may inspect books upon written notice.
Accounting Standards
Management PMR 23

Building Insurance & Excess

  • • Policy Coverage: Body corporate insures all buildings and fixtures against fire, storm, and public liability.
  • • Insurance Excess: Owners are liable for the insurance excess payment if a claim arises from an incident originating inside their section.
Risk & Claims Management
Conduct Rule 4.2

Short-Term Rentals & Occupancy Limits

  • • Minimum Stay: Absolute minimum stay of 2 nights. 1-night bookings/party rentals are strictly prohibited.
  • • Occupancy Cap: Maximum of 2 adults per residential section to prevent building overcrowding.
  • • Guest Registration: Tenants/hosts must submit full guest identification details to building security prior to arrival.
  • • Tenant Accountability: Owners remain fully liable for any conduct fines or rule breaches committed by their short-term guests.
Airbnb & Leasing Bylaws
Conduct Rule 5.1

Quiet Hours, Noise & Nuisance

  • • Quiet Hours: Enforced strictly between 23:00 and 08:00 daily. No loud music, shouting, or television noise.
  • • Construction & Drilling: Heavy drilling or hammering is banned on Sundays, public holidays, and outside weekday working hours.
  • • Common Property Ban: Smoking, vaping, and alcohol consumption are strictly banned in all indoor common corridors and lifts.
  • • Disturbance: Residents must not create excessive noise or behavior that interferes with the peaceful enjoyment of neighbors.
Peace & Good Order
Conduct / Security Rule 3.3

Security, Deliveries & Access Tags

  • • Courier Restriction: Food & package delivery drivers (Uber Eats, Mr D, couriers) are not permitted inside building towers. Residents must meet drivers at ground level.
  • • Access Tags & Biometrics: Tags are strictly personal. Cloning, lending, or giving security tags to unauthorized persons results in immediate deactivation and fines.
  • • Visitor Logging: All visitors must register at reception/security upon entry.
Building Access Control
Conduct Rule 2.1

Pets & Domestic Animals

  • • Prior Approval: No pets are permitted in any section without prior written consent from the Trustees.
  • • Leash Control: Approved pets must be kept on a short leash at all times when traversing common property (lobbies, walkways).
  • • Waste Removal: Pet owners must immediately clean up after their pets on common property.
Animal Control Bylaws
Conduct / Maintenance Rule 6.4

Refuse Disposal & Cleanliness

  • • Refuse Chutes / Bins: Household refuse must be properly bagged and disposed of strictly in designated bins or chutes. No leaking bags in corridors.
  • • Bulky Items: Furniture, boxes, or rubble must not be left in common hallways or lift areas.
  • • Littering: Throwing cigarette butts, bottles, or trash from windows or balconies is strictly prohibited.
Hygiene & Waste Management
Conduct Rule 7.2

Parking Bays & Vehicles

  • • Bay Usage: Vehicles must be parked strictly within marked bay boundaries. No oil leaks or abandoned vehicles allowed.
  • • Speed Limit: Maximum speed within parking garages is 10 km/h.
  • • Visitor Parking: Unauthorized parking in designated visitor bays will result in wheel clamping or towing at owner's risk and expense.
Garage & Vehicle Regulations
Enforcement Rule 8.1

Penalties, Fines & CSOS Conciliation

  • • Breach Fines: Trustees may levy reasonable financial penalties on owners/tenants after written notice and opportunity for a hearing.
  • • CSOS Escalation: Unresolved disputes may be referred to the Community Schemes Ombud Service (CSOS) for formal adjudication.
Compliance & Dispute Resolution
